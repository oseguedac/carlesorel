Oct 13, 2026

Gemini ‘Skills’ replace ‘Gems’

Google is replacing Gemini's custom assistants (Gems) with ‘Skills’ — reusable instructions you summon inside any chat by typing ‘/’. Your existing Gems convert automatically in November.

Affects: Chapter 4C (How to Use Gemini)

Oct 9, 2026

Gemini's free tier drops to Flash-Lite only

Free Gemini app users now get only the smallest model, Flash-Lite. Flash and Pro require a paid plan. If you use Gemini free, expect to see Flash-Lite in the model picker.

Affects: Chapter 4C (How to Use Gemini)

Oct 8, 2026

Google launches a ‘Gemini agent’ for work

A single AI agent that plans tasks across Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Microsoft 365, and Slack — the third move in the agent wave after OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse. Aimed at business users for now.

Affects: Chapter 4D

Oct 7, 2026

SynthID Detector opens to everyone

Google's tool for checking AI watermarks in images, video, and audio is now public at synthid.com. Honest limit: it only catches watermarks from participating systems — it is not a general fake detector.

Affects: “Spot the Scam” chapter

Claude Haiku 5.5: cheaper and more capable

Anthropic's new budget model costs roughly 75% less to run than its predecessor and handles a 1-million-token context window — built for high-volume work like summarization.

Two cautionary tales about AI agents
